# Case Study on a Paper's Dataset
### _Done on Jupyter Notebook with Python_

This repository contains the main notebook and the necessary dataset file. The dataset is taken from the paper of Emille Villacerat and Celbert Himang of Cebu Technological University, titled "Data on behavioural intention to use AI copilot through TAM and AI ecological education policy lens" last May 13, 2025. The dataset contains responses that pertain to behavioral intention, gathered through a quantitative survey, where faculty and students of Cebu Technological University rated with a five point Likert Scale. These responses are assessed to determine the perceptions of AI Copilot adoption among the respondents, with survey questions carefully curated based on existing literature on technology acceptance and educational frameworks.

The notebook first goes over the data preprocessing of the dataset, performing some exploratory data analysis to extend the initial removal of certain responses to remove other responses that are deemed careless/invalid. With the updated cleaned dataset, the research question on the validity of the paper's claims of the dataset is asked. Data modeling is done to replicate the model constructed in the paper. Finally, the same tests, tables, and statistical inferences made by the paper are recreated, now done with the updated dataset. Results conclude that while the quality of the paper's original dataset is questionable, the findings made by the paper are reflected with the updated dataset. Regardless, rigorous data cleaning and verification are still essential to ensure the reliability, transparency, and reproducibility of empirical research.
